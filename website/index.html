Welcome

A little slice of coast, minutes from the city

Tucked into Mjimwema on the Kigamboni shore, Pweza Beach Resort is the place locals and travellers come to slow down. The rhythm here is the tide: unhurried mornings, long lunches, and sunsets that people plan their evenings around.

We keep things warm and relaxed. Whether you are celebrating, catching up with friends, or simply finding a quiet corner with a view, Pweza is designed to make you feel at home by the water.

  • Direct Indian Ocean setting
  • Relaxed beachfront dining and drinks
  • Live events and celebrations
  • A short hop from central Dar es Salaam
